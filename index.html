Privacy Policy for NewsBot

Last updated: 24.09.2026

1. Information We Collect

This bot collects and stores the minimum data necessary to function:

  • Your Telegram Chat ID (used to send you news and identify your session)
  • Your preferred daily news delivery time, if you set one using /setdailytime
  • Temporary message IDs of messages the bot sends you, used only to support the "Clear Chat" feature

We do not collect your name, phone number, contact list, or any other personal information beyond what Telegram automatically provides to any bot you interact with.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your Chat ID and saved preferences are used solely to:

  • Deliver news content you request
  • Send your scheduled daily news digest at your chosen time
  • Allow you to clear bot-sent messages from your chat

3. Third-Party Services

This bot fetches news articles from NewsAPI (newsapi.org). When you search for news or request a category, your query is sent to NewsAPI's servers to retrieve relevant articles. No personally identifying information about you is shared with NewsAPI.

4. Data Storage

Your daily time preference is stored locally in a configuration file on this bot's server. Message tracking data is kept only temporarily and is cleared once you use the Clear Chat function.

5. Data Retention

We retain only the data necessary for the bot to function. You may stop using the bot at any time by blocking it in Telegram, after which no further messages will be sent to you.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your stored preferences at any time by contacting the bot administrator.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
